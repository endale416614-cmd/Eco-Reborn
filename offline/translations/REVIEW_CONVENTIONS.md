Preserve exact IDs, source cells, slash commands, formatting tokens and token order. Keep line breaks at meaningful sentence boundaries; appending unused $r at the end is not a faithful translation. Preserve source padding/markers where present, and add necessary English spaces for known suffix fragments. Do not infer unstated subjects or reverse required/prohibited conditions. If a fragment would move an inserted name/count, hold it outside en pending assembly evidence. Proper names without verified English canon need notes. Identical source/English cells do not count as translations. Status remains draft until actual display/layout is verified.

Only batches listed (and SHA-pinned) in reviewed-batches.json are installed. Use explicit reviewed inputs in archive builds. Never patch commands, numeric rules, save IDs or gameplay to accommodate translations.
