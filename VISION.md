# Project Vision

My goal is to make Emil Chronicle Online fully playable again and give players full control over their game,
characters and progress.

## 1. Fully Playable

- NPCs, shops, monsters, combat, quests, skills and jobs, then dungeons and the rest of the world.
- Rules and data come from the community's SagaECO server and the client's own tables, checked against the real
  client. Content the old server data never had is rebuilt from the client tables.
- Top priority before anything else.

## 2. English

- The whole game readable in English: interface and item names first, then descriptions, quests and NPC dialogue.
- Translations stay tied to the exact Japanese source text, so they can be reviewed and fixed later.

## 3. Quality of Life

- Built for solo play: boosted EXP, drop and gold rates (adjustable), a cash shop where every item is free and
  permanent.
- Community feedback and suggestions are welcome.

## 4. Co-op wLAN RPG (not an MMORPG)

This is not about bringing back an MMORPG. The goal is a co-op RPG you play with friends over wLAN (your home network)
or a server you choose, the way Stardew Valley's co-op works.

In an MMORPG your character lives on someone else's server and disappears when that server shuts down. That is what
happened to ECO in 2017. Here your character is a save file on your own PC, and other people's games are just places
you visit.

- Local saves: your character and progress stay on your PC.
- Bring your character: join a friend's game with your offline character and return home with everything you earned.
- Offline-first: solo play always works without a server or an internet connection.
- Compatibility checks: games verify versions, data and mods before a character joins.

Co-op comes after the game is playable.

## 5. Modding

- Plain, stable data formats for rates, shops, NPC scripts and translations, so players can customize and expand
  their own game.

## The Bigger Picture

I want you to own your game and your own characters. No more losing years of progress because someone else's server
shut down.

Your character stays on your PC. Servers are just places to play together, not where your character's existence
depends on someone else.

**Own your game. Own your character. Keep your progress.**
